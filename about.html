PRIVACY & YOUR DATA

Your data, in plain language

  • Your theme choice, completed modules, best quiz scores, and any name or completion record you create are stored in this browser's local storage. They are not synced to an account. Clearing site data removes them.
  • No account is required to browse or learn. Fonts and icons are requested from a public CDN, and diagrams may load the Mermaid library from jsDelivr when needed. Those services receive normal web request information.
  • If you submit a mentorship request, the fields you provide and your contact consent are sent to the existing Google Apps Script submission service. Do not include passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information.
  • A mentorship request is not a purchase. The form only confirms receipt when the submission service returns a readable acknowledgement. If delivery cannot be confirmed, your entries remain on the page.

For questions about the platform or a mentorship request, contact Pavan on LinkedIn.
