Universal Interview-Clearing Framework

Use this regardless of company. Company cards tell you what to emphasize.

Application Stage

  • Tailor resume for each role using job-description keywords and quantified impact.
  • Prioritize employee referrals and targeted outreach over blind mass applications.
  • Prepare an OA strategy by role: DSA for SDE, SQL/case for consulting-tech roles.

Technical Rounds

  • Start with problem clarification, then state brute force, then optimize with trade-offs.
  • Demonstrate test mindset: edge cases, complexity, failure modes, and debugging approach.
  • For experienced roles, connect solution to production concerns: scale, cost, reliability.

Behavioral and Managerial

  • Prepare STAR stories on ownership, conflict resolution, delivery under pressure, and learning speed.
  • Use business language: customer impact, metrics improved, and trade-offs made.
  • Show collaboration maturity for cross-team and ambiguous problem spaces.

Post-Interview Conversion

  • Send concise follow-up with role fit, strongest round, and enthusiasm for business context.
  • Track round feedback patterns and adjust preparation weekly.
  • Maintain interview journal to reduce repeated mistakes in future rounds.
