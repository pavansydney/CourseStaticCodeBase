1. Which provider is right for you?

Microsoft Azure

Best if your team already uses Microsoft 365, Azure, or Copilot, and you want AI that plugs into that stack.

AWS

Best for the broadest job market and the widest catalog of AI/ML services, from Bedrock to SageMaker.

Google Cloud

Best for data-heavy and ML-focused roles built around Vertex AI, BigQuery, and Gemini.

Salesforce

Best if you work in CRM, Service, or Sales and want to build and deploy Agentforce AI agents.
