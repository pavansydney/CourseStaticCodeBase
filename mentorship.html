GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
before you apply.

Clear expectations make for a better conversation.

Do I need to pay to use Cloudvana?

No. Learning modules and practice questions are free to access. Mentorship is an optional service, with free orientation and focused sessions at 149 INR per session.

What should I use a focused session for?

Bring a specific challenge: choosing a study sequence, understanding a weak topic, planning revision, or deciding what to prioritize next. Specific questions help keep the session useful.

Does applying book a session?

No. We review requests manually and contact you to discuss fit and next steps. Submitting the form does not reserve a time, enroll you, or take a payment.

Is this official provider training?

No. Cloudvana provides independent educational guidance and is not affiliated with certification providers or the employers covered in its interview resources. Check official exam requirements before booking an exam.
